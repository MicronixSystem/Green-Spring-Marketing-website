Who you will be working with.

Our leadership team brings strategic and tactical expertise, so the plan and the execution come from the same people.

Linda Long

Founder and CEO

Linda Long has watched marketing evolve for more than 30 years. Technologies, platforms and tactics have changed dramatically. Human nature has not.

Two principles have guided her work throughout her career: people buy benefits, not features, and they want to understand what’s in it for them.

Linda helps organizations uncover the real value of what they offer, and communicate it in a way that resonates with the people they want to reach. She has a particular ability to take complex products, services and ideas and make their value immediately understandable.

Effective marketing begins with the right brand positioning and messaging: understanding what an organization is really selling, what sets it apart, and why its customers should care.

That perspective is at the heart of Green Spring Marketing: start with what matters to the customer, build the brand around it, and let the marketing follow.
