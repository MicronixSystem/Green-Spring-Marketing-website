## What each frame actually contains

- **Hero.** A solitary broad-crowned tree silhouetted against a sunrise over a
  misty field, palms along the horizon, cool blue sky grading to warm orange at
  the sun. Wide open space on the right for copy. Note: this is not the spreading
  oak shown in the mockup.
- **Acorn.** One acorn with a textured cap and dried oak leaves on a plain warm
  off-white ground. Very editorial, generous negative space, crops well to any ratio.
- **Leaf.** A single backlit green leaf with sharp veining against a near-black
  ground. Strong enough to carry white type directly. This is the one for the
  dark panel.
- **Bark.** Close-up of rough bark with a shallow depth of field, warm browns
  falling to dark. Architectural and textural rather than literal nature stock.
- **Light through leaves.** Backlit narrow leaves radiating from a stem with a
  blown-out bokeh background. Bright and high key, plenty of movement.
- **Leaf detail.** Backlit leaf cluster shot from below against a bright sky, one
  large leaf in front with pronounced pale veining. Tall portrait frame, suits a
  full-height panel.
- **Mountain valley.** Layered misty ridgelines receding to a pale horizon with a
  conifer line along the bottom. Muted green-grey, very quiet. Sits close to
  Stone `#EDEBE5` in tone, so type over it needs measured contrast.

## Before use

Crop deliberately to the section ratio, grade the seven to one consistent look,
convert to WebP, write real alt text. Four of the seven are tall portrait frames
and will need real art direction to sit in wide sections.

### What each Approach frame actually contains

- **Hero.** Two bare trees in fog, finely branched, silhouetted against a flat
  warm ivory sky. The crowns sit right of centre and the left two fifths is
  clean sky, which is where the headline goes. The phone crop takes the left
  tree alone and stops short of the second trunk.

  A leafy replacement was sourced and rejected. Every strong in-leaf candidate
  was either trunk-dominant, which loses the sculptural canopy the brief asks
  for, or set against a cold blue sky that fights the forest and warm ivory
  palette, and one of them repeated the bark close-up already on the home page.
  This frame is out of season for a client called Green Spring, which is the one
  argument against it, but it is the only option that satisfies the brief on
  canopy and negative space and sits in the same muted, misty family as the
  heroes already approved on the home and about pages. Worth putting to Linda.
- **Step 01.** Close-up of grey bare branches and curling tendrils crossing and
  looping round each other over a soft green blur. Reads as things connecting.
  Graded down to sit with the rest.
- **Step 02.** One amber tree glowing among dark conifers on a misty hillside.
  The only warm shape in the frame. Tight crop drops the village and most of the
  mist; the muted tones are close to the existing bark and acorn frames.
- **Step 03.** Looking straight up through a green leaf canopy, bright
  overcast sky showing through the gaps and a pale sun glow right of centre.
  Graded well down from the original, which was oversaturated. Saved at WebP quality 72 (588 KB), still above 400 KB because the foliage is dense.
- **Step 04.** One grey branch crossing the frame with fresh yellow-green leaves
  and red-tipped buds opening along it, pale blurred ground behind. Very quiet,
  low contrast. Strongest read of continuity of the six.
- **Relationship band.** Gnarled live-oak limbs spreading and meeting overhead,
  hung with moss, patches of blue sky between. Cropped above the red path that
  runs through the original so only canopy and shaded grass remain. Heavily
  desaturated. Saved at WebP quality 72 (763 KB). Busy at the edges, so type should not sit directly on it.

### What each Capabilities frame actually contains

- **Hero.** Low sun and haze through a green beech wood. Dark trunks and the
  sunburst sit on the left, soft rays fall across the middle, and the right half
  is open pale haze with a gravel track curving in at the bottom. Warm and green.
  The haze is the headline space, but it is lit and not fully empty, so test
  contrast. The phone crop keeps the sunburst and the big trunk.
- **01 Brand Strategy.** The mossy base of a large trunk spreading into roots
  across brown beech-leaf litter. Dark green moss, bark brown, shallow raking
  light. Reads as foundation.
- **02 Brand Identity.** One large leaf filling the frame, gold midrib up the
  centre and a fine net of veins across muted green. It is a fiddle leaf fig
  leaf, not a woodland species, but nothing in frame says so. Not on black, so
  it does not echo the older leaf frame.
- **03 Website Design and Development.** An avenue of mature, pollarded trees
  with a tarmac path running to a vanishing point, golden dry grass either side
  and a bench. Strong repeating lines. Warmer and drier than the rest, closer to
  late summer. Saved at WebP quality 78.
- **04 Marketing and Growth.** Green acorns clustered on a spiky-leaved oak
  branch in warm low sun, soft gold-brown background. This is a holm or kermes
  type of oak, not the English oak leaf. A busy frame, so it will want a plain
  caption area beside it.
- **Integrated thinking band.** Backlit green leaves and fine branches
  overlapping against a pale hazy sky, one pale trunk at the right edge. Light,
  calm and wide. Saved at WebP quality 74 (271 KB at 2400). Less sculptural than
  the Approach canopy, deliberately, so it stays light.

### What each Editorial frame actually contains

- **Hero band.** A summer wood of tall dark trunks with a low green understorey
  spreading right across the foreground and small gaps of pale sky behind. Deep
  and quiet rather than sunlit, which suits a band that carries no type: the
  headline sits above it and the standfirst below it. The phone crop takes the
  middle of the frame so the band is not one narrow vertical slice.
- **Lead piece, Brand Strategy.** One broad lime in full leaf standing clear of
  the treeline on a sunlit green hillside, with open meadow in the foreground.
  The clearest read of "distinctive" of everything sourced. It is the only frame
  on the page with blue sky; the saturation is pulled to 0.86 to take the hard
  edge off it, which also lifts the warm cast from the grade.
- **Piece, Marketing and Growth.** Wooded green hillsides receding into morning
  mist with a pale meadow running down the slope. Reads as the long view the
  title asks for. The source is a valley with a village in it: the box is cut
  left and high so no house is in frame. Very light at 40 KB because most of
  the frame is soft mist.
- **Piece, Website Design.** Ivy in leaf against the moulded base of a pale
  stone column, warm cream stone against dark green. This is the brief's
  "refined architectural lines" without a line of office stock in it, and it is
  the one frame on the page that is not a landscape.
- **More perspectives strip.** Fern fronds arching over a narrow woodland
  stream, the water carrying the warm brown of its bed. The first crop sat
  higher in the source, where the frame is all out of focus, and the strip read
  as mud; the box was moved down onto the sharp fronds.
